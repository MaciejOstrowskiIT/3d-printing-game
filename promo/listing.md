## Title
Printworks (PL: Drukarnia)

## Short description (≤ 150 chars)
Start with one 3D printer in a garage and grow a printing empire that ends up mining the asteroid belt.

## Description
Print your first figurine by hand, sell it, buy filament, print again. Soon auto printers hum in the garage, farms of hundreds of printers fill the factory floor, and an AI board runs the company while you research the next leap: printing in orbit.

- Idle / incremental gameplay that keeps going while you are away
- Set prices, ride filament colour trends, take risky bulk orders, fix breakdowns
- A research tree with 27 nodes and automation you unlock step by step
- Patents carry over to the next production line
- 20 achievements, Polish and English

## Controls
Mouse or touch. Space: print a figurine (hold to keep printing).

## Categories / tags
Idle, Clicker, Incremental, Simulation, Management, Tycoon
