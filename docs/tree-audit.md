## Status after R2

Done in R2 (`feat/tree-progression`): 19 nodes got a stage `need` (all single-prerequisite nodes that had none and no
money or figurine cost, plus `franczyza`, `stocznia`; `b2b` raised from 10 to 20 orders), and `row` was recomputed so
every node sits below all of its prerequisites. Two nodes of one branch cannot share a grid cell, so rows are not
literally equal to depth where a branch forks; `tests/tree.test.ts` enforces `row >= depth` and `row > row of every
prerequisite` instead.

Still open: joins and alternative routes (R3). The "gap under 1 min" list in section 1 shrinks only for nodes whose
`need` the bot had not met yet; several (`b2b`, `kolekcja`, `leasing`) are still bought quickly because the bot meets
the new need early. That is the signal for R3: more conditions of the same kind will not slow research, joining
branches will.

## Proposals

Input for R2 (gates) and R3 (joins). Findings above are from the bot, which buys the cheapest ready node, so a short
gap means "nothing but compute stands in the way", a long gap mostly means compute was the limit. Both are symptoms of the
same shape: single chains where order is the only rule.

**Common fixes**
- Give every node a reason to wait beyond compute: a stage `need` (printers, farms, orders, total figurines) on nodes
  that today have one prerequisite and nothing else (28 of 69, section 2).
- Layout rows are hand-written and 40 nodes disagree with depth (section 5). Derive the row from depth in R2 and add a test.
- Only 6 nodes have 2+ prerequisites and 6 cross links exist, all pointing into Automation or from Production. Sales,
  Design, Orders and Energy have no links out at all (Energy: none in either direction).
- Space has no root: its first node `autobud` needs `replik`, so its depth is 8 to 14 and the "branch" is really the
  tail of Production. That is fine for the phase switch, just do not count it as a root in R2 rules (there are 7 roots, not 8).

**Production** (chain + 3 forks). `leasing` (0.9 min after `hurt`), `hotend` (1.3 min) are quick side picks: add a
`need` on printers (e.g. 8 and 15) so they open with the fleet, not with compute. `prefab` and `nocna` already have
needs. Join candidate: `corexy` also requires `baterie` (Energy), since a faster motion system draws more power.

**Sales** (chain + 4 forks, 2 links out). `kolekcja` (0.4 min), `franczyza` (0.3 min), `b2b` (0.1 min) open almost
together once `marka`/`eksport` are bought. Add `need` on sold figurines / orders. Join candidates:
`marketplace` also requires `chmura` (Energy, cloud hosting); `eksport` also requires `kurier` (Orders).

**Automation** (3 joins, the best linked branch). `autopkt` and `autooffer`/`autodaily` have one prerequisite and no
`need`: add a `need` (reputation spent, offers taken, daily orders done). Keep as the template for joins elsewhere.

**Space** (14 deep). Late nodes `stocznia` (0.5 min), `lustra` (0.0 min), `regolit` (0.6 min) have gaps near zero
because the only limit is figurines, which the bot has in bulk. Add `need` on drones and factories, as `regolit`
already does. Join: `rojai` also requires `chmura` (compute at scale).

**Materials & quality** (5 deep, 1 join). `mieszanki` (0.9 min) and `serwis` (2.9 min) are quick; `chemia`
(16 min) and `kontrakt` wait only for compute. Add `need` on breakdowns survived or total figurines to `serwis` and
`chemia`. Join: `granulat` also requires `taryfa` or `baterie` (Energy), since pellet extruders need power.

**Design** (5 deep, 1 join). `parametr`, `podpory`, `studio` wait 8 to 19 min only for compute. Add `need` on models
owned (3 and 5). Join: `studio` also requires `marketplace`/`agencja` (Sales), customers drive what to design.

**Orders & logistics** (6 deep, no joins). `negocjator` (27 min), `paczkomaty` (12 min) both hang off `crm`. Make
`paczkomaty` require `crm` and `agencja` (Sales); `dronydost` require `hub` and `autodaily` (Automation).

**Energy & computing** (4 deep, no links). This branch feeds compute for the whole tree and the planned quantum
computer, but nothing requires it. Make it a hub: `serwer` also gates `chmura`; `baterie` is required by `corexy`
and `granulat`; `chmura` is required by `marketplace` and `rojai` (suggestions above). `neuro` and `offline` need a `need`
on total figurines.
